Case Study 1: Managing Operational Risk in Data Operations & Data Load Setup
Scenario
Aventis Finance Pvt. Limited, a regulated financial services entity, operates a highly complex data ingestion ecosystem. Every day, the company processes data from 2,025 client systems into its centralized platform, ‘Datahub’. This data is critical for assessing consumer creditworthiness and is accessed by clients through APIs for downstream credit decisions.

Regulatory norms mandate that all data uploads must be completed within T+3 days, a timeline also embedded in client SLAs. Failure to meet this requirement attracts a penalty of INR 100 per day per file, which can quickly escalate given the large volume of files processed daily.

Beyond financial penalties, delays compromise regulatory reporting accuracy, damage client trust, and expose the organization to reputational risk. Common operational challenges include late client submissions, system outages, unplanned downtime, and resource shortages.

Given the scale and regulatory sensitivity, proactive risk management is essential to ensure compliance, maintain operational resilience, and safeguard the company’s reputation.
Task
The objective is to design a comprehensive risk assessment framework tailored to the data operations and load setup of Aventis Finance. This framework should identify the top five critical risks across dimensions such as People, Process, Technology, Data, Regulatory and External factors.

For each risk, the framework must outline detailed mitigation strategies, including process improvements, system enhancements, and automation opportunities. Additionally, it should define robust monitoring mechanisms with Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), thresholds, and escalation protocols.

The framework should also propose a dashboard for real-time visibility and alerting, enabling proactive intervention before SLA breaches occur. This structured approach will help reduce regulatory exposure, optimize operational efficiency, and strengthen systemic resilience.
